[sighs] Ah. Goosebumps. Cold, or scared? [grumbling] Doesn't matter. Same thing. Almost every hair on your body has a tiny muscle attached to it. When you're cold or frightened, your nerves fire them all at once. Each muscle yanks its hair upright, and the skin bunches up into a little bump. [annoyed] Now, if you were a cat, this would be useful. Fluffed up fur traps warm air, and makes you look bigger to scare off enemies. [shouting] But you are NOT a cat! You have no fur! [annoyed] So you just stand there, covered in tiny bumps, looking exactly the same size. [sighs] Your body is bluffing. With nothing.
